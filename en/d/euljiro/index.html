The story

Euljiro is Seoul’s workshop — printing presses, tile merchants, lighting shops. When rents rose elsewhere, young bartenders and artists moved into the upper floors, changed nothing about the staircases, and put a bar behind a steel door. Locals nicknamed it “Hipjiro”. Half the fun is not being sure you’re in the right building.

A half-day on foot

  1. 1
    15:30Ssanghwa-cha at Eulji Dabang

₩
Open-air beer alleyPhoto menuCards OKOpen late
Insider tip

Come evening, hundreds of drinkers fill plastic tables in the open air. Dried pollack and cold draft beer are still the core order, though fried chicken and stews are on the menu now. Street seating depends on the district office’s permits, so on some nights it isn’t set out.
