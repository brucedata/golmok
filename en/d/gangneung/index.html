Gangneung Myeongju-dong

명주동

Korea’s coffee capital, in alleys of tiled-roof houses near the sea.

StationGangneung stn (KTX)
On footOld town: 20 min radius
Best timeMorning to sunset

The story

Two hours from Seoul by KTX, Gangneung grew Korea’s first real coffee culture. In Myeongju-dong, the old administrative quarter, baristas brought tiled-roof houses and a rice mill back to life as cafes — and the old town has been picked for a five-year national program, running from 2026, to revive its commercial district. Have your coffee in the alleys, then take a bus to Anmok Beach, where cafes line the sand.

A half-day on foot

  1. 1
    10:30Jang-kalguksu at Beoljip
    One dish only, ₩9,000, and they close when the ingredients run out. Closed Tuesdays — and since the cafe Bongbong Bangagan is closed Sundays and Mondays, this route works Wednesday to Saturday.
  2. 2
    11:30Gangneung Daedohobu Gwana
    Free to walk into, 180 meters from the coffee alley, with the national-treasure gate of Imyeonggwan on the grounds.
  3. 3
    12:15Coffee at Bongbong, then the archive
    Hand-drip coffee in the old rice mill. Myeongju Sarangchae is 70 meters away and closes for a midday break from 12:00 to 13:00, so head over after that.
  4. 4
    14:00Gangneung Jungang Market
    A short walk east — about 640 meters as the crow flies. Street snacks on the ground floor, a fish market below, restaurants upstairs.

Gangneung Jungang Market강릉중앙시장

₩
Covered market, three levels
Insider tip

Three levels: a fish market in the basement, dried seafood and street snacks on the ground floor, restaurants upstairs. It is the biggest octopus market in the Yeongdong region, the east side of Gangwon, and the dakgangjeong got famous off a TV show.

Imdang-dong Catholic Church임당동성당

FREE
1955 Gothic church, listed heritage
Insider tip

Built in 1955 and listed as national heritage in 2010 — a pointed bell tower, arched windows, buttresses, and stained glass by Father Cho Kwang-ho inside. Gangneung’s oldest parish.
