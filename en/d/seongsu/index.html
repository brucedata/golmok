Seongsu

성수동

Shoe factories turned into Korea’s coolest cafes and pop-ups.

StationSeongsu / Ttukseom (Line 2)
On foot~20 min radius
Best timeWeekday 11:00–18:00

The story

Until the 2010s this was a working district of handmade-shoe factories and warehouses. The factories never fully left — they just share the streets now with roasteries, galleries and one-week brand pop-ups. That mix of red brick, forklifts and flat whites is exactly why Koreans call it their Brooklyn. Come on a weekday if you can: on weekends the neighborhood gets loved a little too much.

Insider tip

The market has stood in its current form since 1962 and has 139 units. Long-running shops sit beside ones opened by young merchants through a 2016–18 youth-merchant program, and as Seongsu’s commercial scene has grown, some units have shuttered and now stand empty.
